# Demo Script

**Duration**: 3-5 minutes

## Example Questions to Demonstrate
1. **Basic SELECT**: "Which stocks are in the Technology sector?"
2. **ORDER BY & LIMIT**: "Which stock has the highest current price?"
3. **Filtering**: "Show all pending buy orders."
4. **JOIN**: "Which investor has the highest portfolio value?"
5. **Aggregate / GROUP BY**: "Show the total number of shares held per sector."
6. **HAVING**: "Show investors with more than 100 shares of a single stock."
7. **Time-based**: "Show today's trades."
8. **Complex JOIN**: "List all completed trades for AAPL with the investor's name."
9. **Analytics**: "What is the average execution price for TSLA trades?"
10. **Nested/Advanced**: "Which stock had the most price fluctuation today?"
